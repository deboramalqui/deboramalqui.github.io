Components

One system for every screen

The first version had the right pieces (cards, menus, backdrop, app bar) but mixed sizes, a pure-black contained card and a flat blue menu with no elevation. The final version keeps the museum blue and the façade backdrop, and applies one spec to every component. Measurements and states follow Material Design 2 (Roboto, 8 dp grid); the shapes, motion and icons are the museum’s own: arched windows, square edges, thin line icons, a quiet ink wash when you press.
