MAC: booking a school visit to the museum, with the paperwork included

A reservations app for the Museo de Arte Contemporáneo (Universidad Nacional del Litoral, Santa Fe). Research, personas, usability tests and the final screens.

Project
MAC UNL · educational visits app
Role
UX Designer (research → mockups)
When
2022
Method
Interviews, personas, journeys, wireframes, 2 usability rounds
01 — Problem

Teachers can’t book what they can’t justify

Schools in Santa Fe need a formal project, approved by the Ministry of Education, before they can take students anywhere, and it must be filed 30 days ahead. The museum’s booking happened by phone, so teachers never had the information the project needs: exhibition content, duration, capacity, accessibility, schedule.

Persona 1
Claudia, vice-principal

Runs a large school with many groups and time slots. Needs the full logistics to plan one outing for several classes.

Persona 2
Brenda, social worker

Visits with adults with intellectual disabilities. Needs to know if the content fits and if the building is accessible (ramps, wheelchairs, sign language).

Goal
Book and download

Reserve a guided visit and leave with a downloadable file that already contains everything the project form asks for.

02 — Research findings

What two rounds of testing changed

Round 1
  • Exhibition info must show: general description, dynamics, works, price, suggested age, duration, rules.
  • Museum info must show: façade, floor plan, history, staff, purpose, why visit, hours.
  • Download not only the confirmation, but the whole exhibition brief, organized.
Round 2
  • A welcome screen with the façade photo, before login and onboarding, so people recognize the building.
  • Date and time as a direct slot picker plus a calendar of available dates, instead of typed text.
  • Accessibility: proper contrast, caps/contrast toggles, no swipe-only transitions, screen-reader support.
03 — Components

One system for every screen

The first version had the right pieces (cards, menus, backdrop, app bar) but mixed sizes, a pure-black contained card and a flat blue menu with no elevation. The final version keeps the museum blue and the façade backdrop, and applies one spec to every component.

05 — Accessibility

Accessibility, built in

Contrast

Primary #0277BD on white is 4.8:1 (and white on primary the same), which passes AA for normal text. Body text at 87 % black is 16:1.

No swipe-only actions

Tabs and calendar work by tap and keyboard; nothing depends on a gesture, as the round-2 findings asked.

Screen readers

App bar, tabs and bottom nav use native roles; the dialog is labelled; progress reports its value.
