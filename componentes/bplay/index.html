01 — The flow

Six screens, one promise

The sign-up collects only what the platform needs to personalize the lobby, and uses the wait after registration to say the things a licensed operator must say.

02 — Foundation

Dark theme by the book

The first screens used brand green on near-black with ad-hoc spacing. The final design puts them on a dark theme: #121212 surfaces, elevation shown with white overlays, a desaturated primary that passes 4.5:1 on dark surfaces, and text at 87 / 60 / 38 % emphasis.

03 — Components

Every component, every state

Each component follows one spec: 36dp buttons with 4dp corners, 56dp text fields with floating labels, 48dp touch targets on selection controls, state layers at hover 4 % / focus 12 % / pressed 10 %. Hover, tab through and click them.

05 — Accessibility

Accessibility, built in

Contrast

Primary on #121212 is 11.0:1; body text at 87 % white is 14.2:1; 60 % white is 7.2:1. The old brand green on black passed, the thin grey captions did not.

Targets and focus

Every control sits in a 48dp target. Focus is visible as a 12 % state layer, keyboard order follows the stepper, and the dialog traps focus.

Motion

Ripples and the indeterminate bar stop under prefers-reduced-motion. The loading screen is determinate, so a screen reader can announce progress.
