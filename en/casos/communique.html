Six brands, every medium: campaign design at a Maltese agency

At Communiqué I led design for social, web and Google and Meta campaigns for more than 20 brands, from the billboard on the road to the post in the feed. Six campaigns for six brands show how each idea travels through outdoor, print, social and web.

Role
Head Designer. I wrote the briefs and supervised three interns.
Year
2021–2024
Stack
OutdoorTransportPrintSocialWebVideo
Where
Communiqué Ltd, Malta agency
Brands I show
Pakkett, Mr. Riley, BICC, RGF, peeple, NSC

Brief, concept and every format

A campaign at Communiqué starts with the client's brief and ends in a set of pieces for each medium the client buys: billboards and buses, leaflets and brochures, Facebook and Instagram posts, web banners and short video. My job was to turn the concept into a system that holds across all of them, and to brief and review the interns who produced the adaptations.

A campaign is not a key image. It is the same idea surviving a 12-meter billboard and a 1,080-pixel square.

Pakkett French Fries: “Crunch”

A snack launch. The crunch is the product, so the crunch is the campaign: bold yellow and red, the fries breaking the frame, Maltese text on the road and short lines on the phone. Outdoor, social media and a video layout.

Mr. Riley: “Snack Attack” art contest

A consumer activation for Mr. Riley snacks: an art contest promoted on social media and on a landing page, with a visual system for three stages: the call for entries, the “vote now” and the winners announcement.

BICC Skill Card: a card for construction workers

Campaign by the Building Industry Consultative Council for construction workers to ask for the Skill Card. The visual language comes from the building site itself: hazard-tape yellow and black, workers in reflective vests and the card as the protagonist. Billboard, Facebook, leaflet, poster, letter and newspaper ad, with Maltese text.

RGF: “Take Action”

Awareness campaign for the Responsible Gaming Foundation. The “Take Action” poster stops you on a bus, in a brochure and in the feed; the web page mockup, the posts and the merchandising follow the same grid and the same color blocks.

peeple: a recruitment brand from scratch

Identity and launch of peeple, a recruitment agency: logo, brandbook (typography, color, logo construction), A1 posters, brochures and a social media system built on the idea “Borderless talent / Matching talent”.

Ongoing content and the agency's voice

Besides the launches: a testimonial series for NSC on Facebook, awards campaigns with a content calendar (EPALE Malta Awards 2023) and the print and recruitment pieces of Communiqué itself.

What I took away

That is where I moved from product design to marketing: I learned to design for a media plan, to write a brief that an intern can execute, and to keep a single coherent idea from the roadside billboard to the paid post. It is the foundation of the campaigns I run today with measurement.
