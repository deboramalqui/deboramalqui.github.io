Campaign Suite: the dashboard for the whole team to see what each campaign brings in

A product I designed custom for Nido Accesorios. It reads GA4, Search Console, Google Ads and Meta and shows the whole team, from the CEO to each department, how much each campaign cost and what it brought in, compared with similar campaigns.

The idea

A business owner and their team need to decide how much to invest in ads by looking at a single screen. The suite brings the platforms together and applies its own measurement criteria, so every number is comparable and true.

  • A single result type per goal. Each campaign is measured by what it was after: reach, clicks, applications or sales.
  • Comparison between campaigns of the same type. Each one is seen against similar ones, with a verdict in plain words and an editable margin.
  • A common 28-day window, printed on every card and every table.
  • Three roles (view, edit, sync) and read-only access to the platforms.

Data checks

Before showing a dashboard, the suite checks that the numbers are true. In the real work it detected three measurement errors that became rules:

What it detectedWhat it does now
Meta contacts counted five times: 482 in the tool versus about 97 in the ad accountA single result type according to each campaign's goal
A Tag Manager tag in a loop: 1,531 events from 3 peopleDiscards events repeated hundreds of times per person
Meta at 90 days compared with Google at 28 on the same screenA common window, written on screen
