EPALE Malta Awards 2023: an awards campaign with a content calendar

The fourth edition of Malta's adult-learning awards, run by the Ministry for Education. From Communiqué I built the Facebook campaign: the awards' identity, the pieces and a calendar that follows every stage, from the opening of nominations to the finalists.

Role
Campaign designer, at Communiqué. Identity, pieces and content calendar.
Year
2023
Stack
FacebookContent calendarIdentityAds
Client
Directorate for Research, Lifelong Learning and Employability, Ministry for Education, Malta

Three awards, one call

The EPALE Malta Awards recognise the work of adult learners and educators. In 2023 three were given: best adult learner, best adult educator and best innovative adult-learning initiative or project in Malta. The campaign had to explain the three, open nominations and keep interest alive until the deadline on 21 July, then present the finalists in September.

The official call on the EPALE platform

The calendar, stage by stage

Every post has its date relative to the stage, a title, the full text and the instruction for the image or animation. The interns could produce each piece without asking.

Every post shares the campaign hashtags (#EPALEMAs2023, #celebratinglearning, #adultlearning) and the Ministry and Erasmus+ signatures.

What I took away

  • A calendar written with relative dates (“one day before”, “opening day”) survives any date change from the client.
  • When every stage has its piece, the campaign never runs out of content between opening and closing.
