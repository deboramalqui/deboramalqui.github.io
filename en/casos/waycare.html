Waycare: a filterable data table for an AI traffic platform

UX/UI design internship at Waycare Technologies, in Tel Aviv. I redesigned the incident log table of its traffic management platform for smart cities.

Role
UX/UI designer (intern). Workflow mapping, interaction design and interface design.
Year
2019
Stack
SketchBehavior specificationInteraction design

The problem

Waycare's platform uses AI to manage traffic in smart city operations. Its back office includes an incident log: a dense, filterable table with a long history of shifts and reports.

What I did

I mapped the workflow around the table and redesigned it in Sketch, with a 16-page behavior specification for engineering. The redesign has five pieces:

  1. Columns in named groups, and a view panel to save a workspace.
  2. Free-text filters that turn into chips.
  3. A period selector with two modes: period and single day.
  4. One-letter status keys on each row.
  5. A toolset per row, with change history and a save-or-review dialog.

The full case

The specification and the working files from that time are in the published case study, in English. It is where I started in UX/UI.
