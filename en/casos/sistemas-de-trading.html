End-to-end automation: three systems that watch markets and send alerts on their own

Three Python systems that automate market tracking: written rules, email alerts and a review after every close. They run on paper or only suggest: the final decision is mine.

Role
Design of the systems, the rules and the validation. I developed the code with AI assistants, under my direction.

What it automates

Following several markets by hand, with rules that change from day to day, doesn't scale. The systems apply written rules, send email alerts, review themselves after every close and don't execute a real order without a decision from me.

The three systems

SystemWhat it does
Paper botWeekly screening of the universe, evaluation across several timeframes, simulated trades and a daily email.
Accumulation coreFor five large assets: confluence zones, buy tranches, confirmed breakout and monthly allocation. It suggests by email.
VigíaAlerts on a real portfolio by reading the API in read-only mode: stops, conviction, candidates and status.

How it works

How it works unsupervised

  • Eight scheduled jobs synchronized with the daily candle; the monthly ones run on the 1st.
  • State with atomic writes and file locks, and retries that don't duplicate. A network error is not mistaken for “no signal”: on the third failure in a row, a warning email arrives.
  • A three-layer code audit: code auditors, verifiers who tried to refute each finding, and experts. Everything that surfaced was fixed in groups, each with its own commit.
  • A reviewer that proposes and doesn't edit. It evaluates each trade at +2 and +7 candles and requires a minimum sample per group before suggesting a threshold.
  • A summary by email every day, with what happened and what to look at.
