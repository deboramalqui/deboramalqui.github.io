Adidas Pulse: why AI couldn't see adidas.com

Course executive proposal (MA in strategic and digital advertising, Ariel University, Digital Assets course): an SEO 2.0 + AIO strategy for adidas.com to regain visibility in search engines and AI assistants. It is not a commission from Adidas.

Role
I led the SEO/GEO front within a team, coordinating with the leads for social and paid media.
Year
2026
Stack
SEOGEO / AIOE-E-A-TJavaScript renderingKeywords

The situation

adidas.com was losing visibility in AI search channels because of technical JavaScript rendering barriers, while the live messaging stayed focused on discounts.

What I did

  • I audited the JavaScript rendering problems that kept adidas.com out of the emerging channels.
  • I designed an E-E-A-T framework with verifiable author profiles, to strengthen the trust and authority of the content.
  • I mapped an opportunity in "striking distance": keywords in positions 11 to 30.
  • I built a system of five keyword campaigns with their budget, and a sneaker search tool with programmatic SEO.

What I estimated

Data pointSource
An opportunity of US$ 1.6 M to US$ 4.7 M in revenue via programmatic SEOcourse project projection
